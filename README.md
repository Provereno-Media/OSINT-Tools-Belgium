# OSINT-Tools-Belgium
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/65/Flag_of_Belgium.svg" alt="Belgium_FLAG"/>

A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Belgium. Most resources and tools have versions in Dutch and French, and many also in German and English; regional services (Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels) usually exist only in the regional language. Since the CJEU judgment of 22 November 2022, the UBO register is open only to persons who demonstrate a legitimate interest (Royal Decree of 8 February 2023). The cadastral map (CadGIS) is free, but owner data requires a paid, motivated extract from FPS Finance.

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [data.gov.be](https://data.gov.be/) — federal open data portal with datasets from Belgian public bodies. Shows a CAPTCHA to automated visitors. *Lang: NL, FR, DE, EN.*
- [Statbel](https://statbel.fgov.be/) — Belgian statistical office: population, economy, real-estate prices, first names and surnames statistics; tables in the be.STAT database. *Lang: NL, FR, DE, EN.*
- [Nationale Bank van België — Statistieken (National Bank of Belgium — Statistics)](https://www.nbb.be/nl/statistieken) — central bank statistics: financial accounts, balance of payments, interest rates, credit and business surveys. *Lang: NL, FR, DE, EN.*
- [Belgisch Staatsblad / Moniteur belge (Belgian Official Gazette)](https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/cgi_tsv/rech.pl) — official gazette with legislation and, in its annexes, all publications of companies and associations (incorporations, appointments, statute changes). Shows a CAPTCHA to automated visitors. *Lang: NL, FR, DE.*
- [JUPORTAL](https://juportal.be/home/welkom) — public database of Belgian case law, in place since 2017; its search engine replaced Jure-Juridat in November 2020. *Lang: NL, FR, DE.*
- [FSMA — Data portal (Financial Services and Markets Authority)](https://www.fsma.be/nl/data-portal) — FSMA data on supervised entities, plus warnings, administrative sanctions and a check of authorised providers. *Lang: NL, FR, EN.*
- [Brussels Open Data](https://opendata.bruxelles.be/pages/home/) — open data portal of the City of Brussels. *Lang: FR, NL.*

## Legal Entities
- [KBO Public Search / BCE (Crossroads Bank for Enterprises)](https://kbopub.economie.fgov.be/kbopub/zoeknummerform.html) — FPS Economy's free search of all registered entities by enterprise number, name, activity (NACE-BEL), licence or address: status, legal form, address, functions (directors), establishments and links to publications. *Lang: NL, FR, DE, EN.*
- [Balanscentrale — Consult (NBB Central Balance Sheet Office)](https://consult.cbso.nbb.be/) — National Bank's free consultation of annual accounts filed by Belgian companies; needs JavaScript. *Lang: NL, FR, DE, EN.*
- [Companyweb](https://www.companyweb.be/nl) — company information on creditworthiness, payment behaviour, management and PEP/sanctions checks; partly paid. *Lang: NL, FR, EN.*
- [Trends Top](https://trendstop.knack.be/nl/home.aspx) — company data from the business weekly Trends: addresses, key figures, annual accounts, rankings and "Spider" links between entrepreneurs; partly paid. *Lang: NL, FR.*
- [North Data](https://www.northdata.com/) — North Data covers company data in Belgium as part of its XL package, which includes comprehensive information on companies, key persons, and financials. *Lang: EN, DE, FR.*
- [Tesari First Resort](https://tesari.ai/) — Tesari is an all-source intelligence platform designed for high-stakes environments, with applications ranging from trade compliance and supply chain illumination to financial crime investigations and complex litigation. It is a paid platform that offers a Tech for Good (Community Impact) Programme for OSINT practitioners and journalists. *Lang: EN.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [CadGIS (FPS Finance Cadastral Plan)](https://www.minfin.fgov.be/myminfin-web/pages/cadgis) — free online cadastral plan of all parcels in Belgium with boundaries and buildings; search by street and house number. Owner names are not shown: a cadastral extract costs a fee and must be motivated ([conditions](https://fin.belgium.be/nl/particulieren/woning/kadastrale-gegevens)). *Lang: NL, FR, DE.*
- [Geopunt (Flanders)](https://www.geopunt.be/) — Digital Flanders' geoportal with Flemish map layers and address search. *Lang: NL.*
- [WalOnMap (Wallonia)](https://geoportail.wallonie.be/carte) — Wallonia's geoportal viewer combining all regional map layers. *Lang: FR.*
- [BruGIS (Brussels)](https://gis.urban.brussels/brugis/) — urban.brussels map viewer: urban planning and heritage layers for the Brussels-Capital Region. *Lang: FR, NL.*
- [geo.be](https://www.geo.be/) — federal geoportal with geographic information from Belgian federal institutions. *Lang: NL, FR, DE, EN.*

## Vehicles
### Land
- [Car-Pass](https://www.car-pass.be/) — official non-profit for mileage history of used cars sold in Belgium, plus a recall check. *Lang: NL, FR, DE.*

### Maritime
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — live AIS vessel map with vessel and port database. Freemium: live map free; some features paid. *Lang: EN.*
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — international database of ship and shipping-company safety information: IMO number, flag, owner, manager, inspections. Free registration required. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [FPS Mobility — Aircraft registration](https://mobilit.belgium.be/en/aviation/flying-operating/registration) — Belgian Civil Aviation Authority's information on the OO- aircraft register. Shows a CAPTCHA to automated visitors. *Lang: NL, FR, EN.*
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live and historical aircraft tracking without filtering of blocked aircraft; search by registration, ICAO hex or callsign. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Cumuleo](https://www.cumuleo.be/) — database of over 1.46 million mandates held by over 37,400 politicians and senior officials since 2004, built on Court of Audit lists: cumulated mandates and remuneration, missing declarations. *Lang: FR, NL.*
- [Rekenhof — Mandaten (Court of Audit — Mandate Lists)](https://www.ccrek.be/nl/mandaten) — annual lists of mandates and remuneration of public office holders and lists of those who failed to file; asset declarations are kept sealed and are not published. Shows a CAPTCHA to automated visitors. *Lang: NL, FR, DE.*
- [De Kamer — Kamerleden (Chamber of Representatives — Members)](https://www.dekamer.be/kvvcr/showpage.cfm?section=/depute&language=nl&cfm=cvlist54.cfm) — the 150 members of the Chamber with profiles, groups and substitutes. *Lang: NL, FR.*
- [Verkiezingsresultaten / Résultats électoraux (Election Results)](https://resultatselection.belgium.be/) — FPS Interior's official election results. *Lang: NL, FR, DE.*
- [Advocaat.be — Zoek een advocaat (Flemish Bar — Find a Lawyer)](https://www.advocaat.be/zoek-een-advocaat) — search of lawyers registered with the Flemish bars. *Lang: NL.*
- [AVOCATS.BE](https://avocats.be/) — Order of the French- and German-speaking bars, with access to the bars' lawyer directories. *Lang: FR, DE.*
- [AGATHA (State Archives of Belgium)](https://agatha.arch.be/en/) — search environment of the State Archives (replaced search.arch.be in 2024): genealogical sources such as civil and parish registers, analyses of records and archive inventories. *Lang: NL, FR, DE, EN.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [e-Procurement (BOSA)](https://www.publicprocurement.be/nl) — federal e-procurement platform with the Belgian Bulletin of Tenders: notices of federal, regional and local contracting authorities; needs JavaScript. *Lang: NL, FR, DE, EN.*
- [Marchés publics en Wallonie](https://marchespublics.wallonie.be/home.html) — Walloon public procurement portal: search of Walloon tenders and guidance for bidders. *Lang: FR.*
- [TED — Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/) — EU procurement-notice search; filter by country Belgium for above-threshold notices and contract awards. *Lang: EN, multi.*

## WHOIS
- [DNS Belgium — WHOIS](https://www.dnsbelgium.be/en/whois) — official .be registry lookup of domain registration details. *Lang: NL, FR, EN.*
- [IANA — .be Delegation Record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/be.html) — official delegation data for .be: registry operator DNS Belgium, contacts and WHOIS server `whois.dns.be`. *Lang: EN.*
- [RIPE Database Query](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — RIPE NCC WHOIS for IP ranges and AS numbers allocated to Belgian networks. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
